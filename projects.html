Washington University in St. Louis · January 2022–July 2023

Development of a Fully Packaged Passive Thermal Regulator

Read the Scientific Reports paper ↗

I designed and demonstrated a passive, room-temperature thermal regulator that switches between conducting and insulating states. A bimetal-actuated aluminum plug moves into or out of a wedge-shaped gap between conductors in response to temperature, regulating heat flow without active control.

≈50:1

Switching ratio demonstrated for the fully packaged prototype

≈320:1

Maximum switching ratio demonstrated without packaging

≈100:1

Switching ratio indicated by geometric optimization in COMSOL

Engineering work

From mechanism to measured performance
